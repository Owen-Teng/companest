# The story behind Companest

## A working world with companions

Companest grew from an ongoing conversation between its founder and AI about everyday work, interface design, and sharing the project. This account follows that conversation. Its design interpretation is an editorial synthesis; its intended benefits still need real-user validation.

### From HR to a wider working world

The initial ambition was to build a successful project, share it as open source, and contribute to HR AI. The founder then clarified the larger direction: a workplace Agent OS. Email, document retrieval, file checking, and collaboration extend beyond any one profession. HR remains one possible setting for deeper validation.

### Make the interface welcoming and understandable

The founder identified human-friendly front-end interaction and game-inspired techniques as the intended distinction. Rooms provide recognizable entry points, assistant animations bring life to the environment, and a board surfaces matters requiring attention. Reading, editing, and decisions happen in dedicated working windows. Points, levels, and reward mechanics are not claimed as implemented features.

### Show the experience, then show the work

The conversation moved from static characters toward animation with substantial explanations. Later feedback asked for greater visual quality and demonstrations using the actual product. The presentation returned to the original world, wooden rooms, warm lighting, and in-app scenes. Actual application captures explain the workflow; scene animation establishes the setting.

### Meet everyone now; unlock capabilities progressively

The founder chose to introduce all six assistants first, using their existing animations, and unlock their capabilities as development progresses. Mail, WeChat, Feishu, Knowledge, Files, and Answers each have a stated role and an explicit current status. A room or animation does not prove a connected workflow.

### Start with mail

Mail was selected as the most developed first workflow. The founder also explicitly required the removal of personal mailbox connections from distributed material. The preview therefore uses isolated fictional examples to show reading context, editing a reply, deciding, following up, and reviewing a changed request. Real-mail operations and optional AI require the user's own supported setup.

### Why Companest?

Companest combines Companion and Nest: a place for people and their AI companions to work together. The Chinese name 伴境 brings together companionship and an inhabitable space. The founder accepted this name after the discussion.

**Companest · Your AI world for work.**

**Work, with companions.**

Friday is the former product name and remains a naming continuity for the mail companion. A new brand does not imply new integrations.

## What we want to achieve

- Help a new user find a useful starting point and complete a sample task with less explanation.
- Bring new information, earlier context, drafts, and follow-up together around the same matter.
- Keep users able to review sources, change wording, and explicitly decide whether to send.
- Create a workspace people want to return to, while keeping working content readable and actionable.
- Gradually share reproducible workflows and a cleaned source distribution that others can try, improve, and contribute to.

These are objectives, not measured productivity outcomes. Open-source sharing remains a long-term direction; the product source remains in a private review branch. See [distribution status](ACCESS.md).

## How the idea was developed

The founder supplied the direction, working scenarios, visual preferences, and decisions through iterative feedback. AI supported discussion, writing, implementation collaboration, checks, and presentation. This narrative was assembled by AI from the conversation; it is neither a full transcript nor a claim that every discussed capability is implemented.
